# OpenPairings is installed

Start it from the shortcut on your Desktop or in the Start Menu. A small
window opens, waits for the server to answer, and then opens your browser -
nothing to type, no login.

Your tournaments are kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\OpenPairings` under your own
Windows account, separate from wherever OpenPairings itself was installed.
Uninstalling OpenPairings removes the program only - that folder, your
database and your backups included, is left in place.
