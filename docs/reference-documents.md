# FIDE reference documents, and where the local copies are

These are FIDE's own publications, cited across this repository and
Ainalrami's. They are **not committed here**: they are FIDE's copyrighted
material, this repository is public, and a link plus a paraphrase is the
right amount to hold (the same rule `docs/design-fide-mode.md` follows for
the verification checklist). FIDE also moves and withdraws them - the 2025
glossary linked from the TEC downloads page 404s as of 2026-09-12 - so a
local copy is kept outside the repository.

**Local copies:** `C:\Users\jorian\Desktop\fide-handbook\tec\`
(downloaded 2026-09-12; the handbook chapters themselves are in the `pdf\`
folder beside it).
