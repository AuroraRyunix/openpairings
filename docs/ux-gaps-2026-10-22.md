# UX gap audit, 2026-10-22

The same walk Alnasl had the day before (`alnasl/docs/ux-gaps-2026-10-21.md`),
done on OpenPairings: every route in `router.ex` and every LiveView, read for
the lifecycle a person expects of what it shows (make, change, remove with a
confirm that says what goes, undo where it is cheap), plus dead ends, actions
without feedback, stale ids that crash, errors shown as Elixir terms, flows
that need knowledge the page never gives, phone-width overflow, untranslated
Dutch, and the manual out of step with the screen.

Found: about 130 gaps over six slices (a few reported twice by neighbouring
slices). Needs a decision: 24. Left open without a decision needed: 2 (end of
this file). Everything else, about 100, is fixed in this pass.

The headline: an open dialog on the Pairings page could take the page down if
another tab unpaired the round underneath it; a player with played games
could be removed with a one-line English confirm that did not mention their
opponents' games; the header menus on the Players page rewrote every
player's payment or presence in one click with no question and no restore
point; and every missing or foreign tournament id ended on the two words
"Not Found" on a white page.

## Checked and fine

Every `phx-click`/`phx-change`/`phx-submit` in every LiveView has a handler.
Ids from events go through `parse_id`/`Integer.parse` and non-bang lookups
almost everywhere. Tournament delete and purge (typed DELETE, Recycle bin
explained), archive and archive group, leave, team withdraw/reinstate/delete,
draw lots, TPN regenerate, result clearing (second click), match forfeits,
publish level, group create/join/rename/leave, rooms, the FIDE-mode departure
gate, TRF and late-file sends, results-site take-down, account deletion,
revoking boxes and phones from the account page. The main grids scroll on
phones. The NL manual has all twenty chapters and the program pages, with
the same headings; every chapter link and anchor resolves; the NL catalogue
uses "je" throughout (the one "u" quotes Windows' own dialog).

## Needs a decision

| Page | Gap | Options |
|---|---|---|
| Every `/t/:id/...` page | A missing/foreign tournament now gets a friendly 404 page; it could instead go back to the Tournaments list with a flash | (a) the 404 page as now; (b) an on_mount redirect to `/` with a flash; (c) both |
| History, Audit | Times are UTC but not labelled; "Today" turns at UTC midnight | (a) append " UTC" like hand-off times; (b) browser time zone via a hook; (c) a time zone per tournament |
| Live round (`/t/:id/live`) | No top-bar tab lights up; reachable only from a link on Pairings | (a) highlight Pairings; (b) add "Live round" to Advanced |
| Top bar publishing pill | An unknown publishing error shows the raw term (deliberate fallback) | (a) keep; (b) a sentence, the term in a tooltip |
| Tournaments list on a phone | Seven columns in a card that cannot scroll (the More menu needs `overflow: visible`), so Status and More are cut off at 375 px | (a) hide System/Rounds/Dates on narrow screens; (b) one card per tournament; (c) the More menu as a popover so the card can scroll |
| Badge editor | Saves as you type with no Save button and no "saved" sign; the event settings beside it have a Save button | (a) a "saved" tick and a hint; (b) an explicit Save |
| Invitation | Tokens are single-use, so a re-opened link cannot lead to the tournament itself | (a) keep accepted tokens resolvable for their invitee; (b) as now with the new hint |
| Rating lists | A list cannot be renamed | (a) rename keeping sequence references; (b) as now |
| Players | Ratings, birth year and federation are not range-checked (−50, 99999, 3020, "Belgium" all stored) | (a) refuse in the changeset (imports would refuse too); (b) warn on the page only |
| Categories | A category cannot be renamed; remove-and-add loses players' tags, rule and prize count | (a) rename rewriting rules, prizes and every player's tags with a restore point; (b) as now (the remove confirm says what goes) |
| Entries | "Last checked" lives in the socket and is forgotten on every visit | (a) store it per tournament (a column); (b) as now |
| Incidents | Created from phones, never resolved or removed; arbiter calls stay highlighted forever | (a) a "Handled" toggle; (b) delete with a confirm, audit-logged; (c) both |
| Pairings, hand edits | A hand-edit session cannot be discarded as a whole and takes no restore point | (a) "Discard hand edits" back to the session's before-boards while no results exist; (b) a restore point when the session starts; (c) both |
| Pairings, Chess960 | The position can be drawn once only (now behind a confirm) | (a) allow a redraw while the round has no results, audit-logged; (b) as now |
| Pairings, round robin | Redoing a schedule means unpairing round by round from the last | (a) "Unpair the whole schedule" while no results exist; (b) as now (the confirm now says so) |
| Officials | Arbiters must come from the FIDE list; with no list downloaded, officials cannot be saved | (a) the new hint and link only; (b) also accept a typed FIDE ID, flagged unverified |
| Settings, tournament | The tournament format (Swiss / round robin / team) can be changed after rounds are paired | (a) lock hard; (b) lock with Unlock like the pairing system |
| Settings, scoring | Win/draw/loss/bye points change after rounds are paired without a word, re-scoring played rounds | (a) warn as the absence fields do; (b) lock with Unlock |
| Settings pages | Leaving a page with unsaved edits drops them without asking | (a) confirm on leave; (b) auto-save |
| Settings, tournament | A collaborator's role cannot be changed, and a pending invitation cannot be re-sent | (a) role select and Resend; (b) remove and re-invite as now |
| Settings, tournament / Officials | The organiser's name is editable in two places; editing it on Tournament leaves the FIDE id from Officials behind | (a) read-only on Tournament with a link to Officials; (b) clear the id when the name changes |
| Settings, dates | Dates out of order are accepted | (a) warning; (b) refusal |
| Dutch wording | The catalogue drifts: Team/Ploeg, Bordpunten/partijpunten, Opslaan/bewaren, rondes/ronden, e-mail/email, "FIDE-gerated" | Pick one of each; then one pass |
| Manual | 24 screenshot slots per language all show "Screenshot to come" | (a) make them per `priv/manual/SCREENSHOTS.md`; (b) drop the slots until they exist |

- Some labels on the Rooms, FIDE and About settings pages and `Tournaments.refusal_message/2` (archived / handed off) are still English.
