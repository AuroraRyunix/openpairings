# The public results site

OpenResults is where players, parents and spectators follow a tournament on
their own phones. They find the pairings, the results as they come in, the
standings, a cross-table, a card per player, and a form to enter the
tournament. It shows what OpenPairings sends it and computes nothing: the
standings arrive already worked out, in the order the arbiter chose, and a
round that was not published is simply not there.

**When you need it.** Whenever the tournament should be visible to anybody
but the arbiter. You do not install it to publish: connect OpenPairings to a
results site once and choose, per tournament, how public it is. A
federation or a club may also run a copy of its own for its own
tournaments; the licence, at the foot of this page, says so in as many
words.

**Where it fits.** [Publishing](14-publishing.md) sends a tournament there;
[OpenResults: the public results site](15-openresults.md) shows what the
spectators see and what the arbiter controls. With electronic boards,
[Alnasl](/help/other/alnasl) sends the moves to it while the games are
played.
